Gre写作

2026/5/21 Gre Gre

此篇为Gre第一部分写作备考

注意：不要太重视词汇语言

重视思维逻辑

一、文章结构

  • Introduction

  • 3 个 body part

    • claim（正面论证）

      admittedly, xxx（正面） can be beneficial in certain contexts

    • counterclaim（反面驳斥）

      however, xxx（反面） can also be deeply problematic

    • synthesis（折中，分情况讨论）

      taken together, these considerations suggest that an eclectic approach to xxx is essential（折中方案）

  • conclusion

二、案例积累

因果类：discuss the extent to which you agree or disagree with the claim and the reason on which that claim is based

主要立场：部分同意 + 限定条件
1、
题目：

Claim: An attitude of certainty undermines the advancement of knowledge of a subject.

Reason: The more certainty there is about a topic, the weaker the motivation to learn anything new about it.

观点：

“过度的 certainty 有害”，但提出 “适度的 certainty 能推动进步”

结构：
  • Introduction段：

    The claim that（改写claim部分：certainty对知识进步有害）rooted in the idea that （改写reason部分：certainty会降低学习新知识的动机）, holds true in many cases but overlooks contexts where（给出另一方面观点：已有知识带来的自信可以促进进步）

    While overconfidence often leads to intellectual stagnation, moderate certainty can provide the foundation needed to explore new ideas and push fields forward.（最后用while或者although句型给出自己的观点：过度的 certainty 有害，适度的 certainty 能推动进步）

  • 核心段：

    • 支持段：certainty 确实会削弱动力

      Admittedly, the reason given makes intuitive sense.

    • 反对段：certainty 也能推动进步

      However, this view fails to account for（how certainty can fuel knowledge advancement in many fields）

    • 折中段：问题不是 certainty，而是 “绝对的、不接受质疑的 certainty”

      Moreover, some level of certainty is necessary for knowledge to advance at all.

      论证

      Thus, the problem is not certainty itself, but absolute, unexamined certainty.

  • 总结段：

    In conclusion, while excessive certainty can indeed reduce motivation and slow knowledge growth, moderate, flexible certainty is essential for progress. The claim and its reason capture the danger of closed-minded overconfidence but ignore the ways in which foundational confidence drives innovation.
